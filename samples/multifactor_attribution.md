# Multi-Factor Portfolio: 2021-2024 Attribution Review

## 1 Scope

This attribution review covers the live multi-factor sleeve over four calendar
years. It is reproduced here as a chunking stress case: the monthly return table
runs to 48 rows and the industry attribution table to 30, sizes that are entirely
ordinary in a converted research PDF and entirely fatal to a character-window
chunker.

## 5 Conclusion

Active return is dominated by selection within a small number of financial and
cyclical industries; allocation contributes little and is close to noise. The bias
statistic stays inside its confidence band in fourteen of sixteen quarters.
